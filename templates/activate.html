How activation works

  1. Log in with the email used when you paid (or set your password from the email we sent after purchase).
  2. Paste your license key exactly as given — no extra spaces.
  3. Tap Activate. The key is linked to your account email.
  4. Pro/Pro+ features (higher batch limits, clone, music, etc.) unlock only while that account is logged in — not from a browser cookie alone.

Where to find your key

  • Paid by card (Freemius): confirmation email from Freemius after checkout.
  • Paid manually (EasyPaisa/JazzCash/bank): VoxCraft emails your key once payment is verified.
Frequently asked questions
"Invalid license key" — what should I check?

Copy-paste rather than retype, and strip stray spaces. Confirm you're using the key from the correct email (Freemius vs manual payment).

Why do I need to log in for Pro?

Paid plans are bound to your account so Pro+ batch limits and features cannot be shared via cookies or IP alone. Free tools still work without an account.

Can I use one license on multiple devices?

Log in on each device with the same account. Device unlock is only needed if activation is blocked as “already active on another device.”
