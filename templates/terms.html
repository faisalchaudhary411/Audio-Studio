Video redub

Video redub is a Pro feature. It transcribes the speech in your video, translates it, creates a new voice with a stock synthetic voice, and can keep the original background music. These steps are automated and can make mistakes: words may be misheard or mistranslated, timing may need adjusting, and the AI separation of voice and music is best-effort, so faint traces of the original voice may remain and the background may not be preserved exactly. You are responsible for reviewing and, where needed, correcting the transcript and the voiced text before you generate the video, and for checking the result before you publish it.

Each redub run counts toward your daily and monthly redub allowance, and the characters of the text you voice count toward your text-to-speech quota every time you generate. Your review session and generated files are temporary (see the Privacy Policy): download your result promptly, because we do not keep a library of your videos and cannot guarantee that a file will still be available later.

The voices used are synthetic. Where a platform's rules or the law require it, you are responsible for labelling dubbed or AI-generated content as such.
