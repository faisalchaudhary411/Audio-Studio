Audio-only redub keeps the original picture and replaces the spoken track. The pipeline extracts audio, transcribes speech, translates the script, re-voices it with a stock neural voice, then muxes the new audio onto the video — without needing a cloned voice or GPU time.

Built for creators who want an Urdu, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, or English version of an existing clip without re-recording. Uses the same neural voices as Voice Studio.

How it works

  1. Upload an MP4/MOV/WebM/MKV (up to 50 MB, about 10 minutes).
  2. Choose the source language for transcription (or leave Auto).
  3. Pick the target language and any of the 130+ stock voices — same picker as Studio.
  4. VoxCraft transcribes, translates, generates the new voice, and muxes it onto your video.
  5. Download the dubbed MP4, or just the new audio track if you prefer to re-mux yourself.

Tips

  • Clear speech transcribes better than music-heavy or noisy tracks — denoise first if needed.
  • If source and target are the same language, translation is skipped and only the voice is replaced.
  • Match the target voice's language to the target language for natural pronunciation.
  • Long videos use TTS character quota the same way Studio does.
Frequently asked questions
Is this free?

No — redub is included with the Pro plan. Free accounts can still use Transcribe and Extract audio separately.

Does it clone the original speaker?

Not in Phase 1. It uses the same stock neural voices as Studio (130+ voices, 40+ languages). Cloned-speaker redub is a later Pro+ option.

Which languages work?

Any language pair where we have a stock voice and translation coverage — including Urdu, Hindi, Bengali, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Arabic, and English variants.

How long can a video be?

Up to about 10 minutes and 50 MB in Phase 1.
