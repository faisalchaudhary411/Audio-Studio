Redub video audio online with VoxCraft: upload a clip, choose a target language and neural voice, and download a new dubbed MP4 that keeps the original picture. The pipeline extracts audio, transcribes speech, translates the script, re-voices it, then muxes the new track — without requiring voice cloning or a local GPU.

Built for creators who need an Urdu, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Arabic, or English version of an existing explainer without re-recording. Voices come from the same library as Voice Studio. If the source is noisy, clean the track first with Remove background noise or extract audio via Extract audio from video and test transcription quality before a full redub.

Redub is not automatic lip-sync acting and not a clone of the original speaker in Phase 1. It replaces the spoken track with a clear stock neural voice in the target language. That is the right tool when localization speed matters more than matching the original timbre. For same-language retakes in your voice, see Voice cloning (Pro+).

Character usage follows the same TTS quota rules as Studio. Long videos consume more quota — prefer clear speech, moderate length, and a short test clip when you are validating a new language pair.

How it works

  1. Upload an MP4/MOV/WebM/MKV (up to about 50 MB / ~10 minutes in Phase 1).
  2. Choose the source language for transcription (or leave Auto when speech is clear).
  3. Pick the target language and any stock neural voice from the Studio library.
  4. VoxCraft transcribes, translates (when languages differ), generates the new voice, and muxes it onto your video.
  5. Download the dubbed MP4, or take the new audio track only if you prefer to re-mux yourself.

Tips for better redubs

  • Clear speech transcribes better than music-heavy or noisy tracks — denoise first if the room tone is strong.
  • If source and target are the same language, translation is skipped and only the voice is replaced.
  • Match the target voice’s language to the target language for natural pronunciation.
  • Start with a 30–60 second sample before redubbing a full 10-minute lesson.
  • Long videos use TTS character quota the same way Studio does — watch remaining quota on Pro.
  • For script-first narration (no existing video), use Voice Studio instead of redub.

Frequently asked questions

Is video redub free?

No — redub is included with the Pro plan. Free accounts can still use Transcribe and Extract audio separately. See pricing.

Does it clone the original speaker?

Not in Phase 1. It uses the same stock neural voices as Studio (130+ voices, 40+ languages). Cloned-speaker redub is a later Pro+ direction — today, use Voice cloning when you need a specific voice identity on new text.

Which languages work?

Language pairs where we have stock voices and translation coverage — including Urdu, Hindi, Bengali, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Arabic, and English variants. Always test a short clip for your pair.

How long can a video be?

About 10 minutes and 50 MB in Phase 1. Longer projects should be split or handled in a full NLE.

Is lip-sync perfect?

No. This is audio-track replacement, not a full lip-sync performance model. It is ideal when viewers prioritize clear language over pixel-perfect mouth match.

What if transcription is wrong?

Improve the source: less music, less noise, clearer speech. Optionally extract and review text with the transcribe tool before relying on a full redub.

Can I keep background music?

The pipeline replaces the spoken track from the mixed audio path. Music-heavy mixes can confuse transcription — prefer voice-forward sources or separate music in post.

Redub vs generating a new voiceover in Studio?

Use redub when you already have video and need another language on the same picture. Use Studio when you start from a written script and will edit video afterward.
