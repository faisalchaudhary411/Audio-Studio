About VoxCraft

Practical audio tools for people who make things.

VoxCraft is an independently built and operated browser-based studio for text-to-speech, voice tools, transcription and everyday audio editing — with first-class support for Urdu, Hindi and other South Asian languages.

Who runs VoxCraft

VoxCraft is built and maintained by a small independent team, not a large corporation. The product is operated from Pakistan and is focused on the real needs of creators who work in Urdu, Hindi, English and other languages every day. We handle support, licensing, content and product decisions ourselves.

Because we are a small team, we can move quickly on feedback and prioritise the languages and workflows that larger platforms often treat as secondary. If something is broken or missing, you can reach us directly through the contact page or by emailing support@voxcraft.site.

What VoxCraft is

VoxCraft brings common audio tasks into one place so you do not need a different service for every small job. The free tier works without an account. Paid plans remove daily limits and unlock advanced features such as voice cloning and AI music generation.

  • Create narration from text in the Voice Studio (88 neural voices across 27 languages).
  • Transcribe audio into text.
  • Convert between common audio formats.
  • Merge clips, trim and split recordings.
  • Reduce steady background noise.
  • Apply voice effects and extract audio from video.
  • On eligible plans: clone a voice from a short reference clip or generate instrumental music from a text description.

Why we built it this way

Most everyday audio tasks are small and specific: get a clear Urdu or Hindi narration for a YouTube video, clean a short interview, convert a file, or pull the audio track out of a clip. Doing each of those with a different service or a full desktop editor creates unnecessary friction.

Multilingual support was a deliberate priority from the start, not an add-on. Urdu and Hindi narration in particular are often treated as an afterthought by tools built primarily for English. Getting pronunciation, code-switching and natural delivery right for South Asian creators shaped a large part of the product design.

Editorial standards & content

The guides and articles on this site are written to help creators complete real workflows. We aim to describe capabilities and limitations clearly rather than over-promise. Where a guide includes a comparison or recommendation, we explain the criteria so readers can judge for themselves.

Our testing approach for voices and tools is documented on the How we test page. For practical tutorials and deeper guides, see the blog.

Transparency & policies

Frequently asked questions
Is VoxCraft free to use?

Yes. The free tier works with no signup, within daily and monthly usage limits. Pro removes those limits on text-to-speech; Pro+ adds voice cloning and AI music generation. See pricing for details.

Which languages does VoxCraft support?

The Voice Studio currently offers 88 neural voices across 27 languages, with particular attention paid to Urdu and Hindi quality. Check the live voice list inside Voice Studio.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in the browser. There is nothing to download for any of the tools.

Who can I contact for support or business questions?

Use the contact form or email support@voxcraft.site. We read every message and usually reply within a day.

Where is VoxCraft operated from?

VoxCraft is independently operated, with a primary focus on creators in Pakistan, India and the wider South Asian diaspora, while remaining available worldwide.
