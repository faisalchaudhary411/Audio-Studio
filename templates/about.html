About VoxCraft

Practical audio tools for people who make things.

VoxCraft is an independent audio creation platform built around text-to-speech, voice tools, transcription and everyday audio editing.

VoxCraft was created to make common audio tasks easier to access from one place. Instead of requiring a separate service for narration, transcription, conversion and basic editing, the platform brings those workflows together in a simple browser-based studio.

The platform is particularly focused on multilingual voice creation, including Urdu, Hindi and other languages that are often treated as secondary options in creator tools. The voice library, previews and generation workflow are designed to let creators judge a voice for themselves before committing to it.

What you can do with VoxCraft

  • Create narration from text in the Voice Studio, with adjustable voice settings.
  • Transcribe supported audio files into text.
  • Convert audio between common formats.
  • Merge clips, trim recordings and split audio.
  • Reduce steady background noise in recordings.
  • Apply voice effects and extract audio from video.
  • Clone a voice from a short reference clip, or generate music from a text description, with eligible plans.

Why VoxCraft exists

Most audio tasks are small and specific — transcribe this one interview, trim the dead air off this one clip, get a rough narration draft for this one video. Doing each of those with a different service, or a full desktop editor meant for much bigger jobs, adds friction that's out of proportion to the actual task. VoxCraft exists to make that middle ground — tasks too small for a full editor, too specific for a general-purpose tool — fast and free to start.

Multilingual support was a deliberate priority from the start, not an add-on. Urdu and Hindi narration in particular are often treated as an afterthought by tools built primarily for English, with pronunciation quality to match. Getting that right for South Asian creators specifically shaped a lot of the platform's design.

Built around real creator workflows

VoxCraft is designed for YouTube creators, educators, podcasters, developers, freelancers and anyone who needs practical audio processing without installing a desktop editor for every small task.

We aim to describe the capabilities and limitations of the tools clearly rather than promise perfect results. Where a guide includes a comparison or recommendation, the goal is to explain how the result was obtained so readers can make their own informed choice.

Frequently asked questions
Is VoxCraft free to use?

Yes — the free tier works with no signup, within daily and monthly usage limits. Pro removes those limits on text-to-speech; Pro+ adds voice cloning and AI music generation. See pricing for details.

Which languages does VoxCraft support?

The Voice Studio supports 27 languages and 88 neural voices, with particular attention paid to Urdu and Hindi voice quality. Check the voice list in Voice Studio for the current lineup.

Do I need to install anything?

No — everything runs in your browser. There's nothing to download or install for any of the tools.

Who is VoxCraft built by?

VoxCraft is an independently built and maintained platform, not a large company product. That means faster iteration on feedback, but also a smaller team — if something's broken or missing, let us know.
