Questions

Before you start

Does it actually sound natural, or robotic?

VoxCraft uses neural TTS voices, not the older robotic synthesis style — the same underlying voice technology used by major platforms. The best way to judge is to listen: try the samples above, or generate your own script free.

Can I use the audio commercially — YouTube, ads, client work?

Yes. Audio you generate is yours to use, including monetized YouTube videos, client projects, and commercial work.

Do you store or read the scripts I paste in?

Scripts are processed to generate your audio and aren't used for anything beyond that. See the privacy page for the full details.

Do I need to create an account to try it?

No. The free tier works immediately — no signup, no email, no card required to generate your first voiceover. See free text to speech for limits and workflow tips.

Is there free Urdu and Hindi text to speech?

Yes. Urdu and Hindi neural voices are available on the free tier. Dedicated guides: Urdu text to speech and Hindi text to speech.

Can I generate AI music or clone a voice?

Voice cloning and the AI music generator are Pro+ features. Text-to-speech and the free audio tools work on Free and Pro without cloning or music.

How do I make a YouTube voiceover with AI?

Paste your script in Studio, pick a language and voice, generate, download, then trim or merge with free tools. Step-by-step: how to create a YouTube voiceover and text to speech for YouTube.
