Questions

Before you start

Does it actually sound natural, or robotic?

VoxCraft uses neural TTS voices, not the older robotic synthesis style — the same underlying voice technology used by major platforms. The best way to judge is to listen: try the samples above, or generate your own script free.

Can I use the audio commercially — YouTube, ads, client work?

Yes. Audio you generate is yours to use, including monetized YouTube videos, client projects, and commercial work.

Do you store or read the scripts I paste in?

Scripts are processed to generate your audio and aren't used for anything beyond that. See the privacy page for the full details.

Do I need to create an account to try it?

No. The free tier works immediately — no signup, no email, no card required to generate your first voiceover.
