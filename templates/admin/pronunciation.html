Pronunciation Dictionary

Global find/say word substitution, applied to all Studio text-to-speech generation before it's sent to the voice engine — for brand names, acronyms, and commonly mispronounced words.

This works by respelling the word in the text itself (e.g. "Nginx" → "Engine-X") — not true phoneme-level pronunciation control, since the underlying voice engine (edge-tts) doesn't expose that. Matching is whole-word and case-insensitive by default, so "Find: Go" won't affect "Google". Applies to Single and Batch generation, not to voice previews.
