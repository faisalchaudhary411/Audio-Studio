What we collect

Text you submit for speech generation, uploaded audio files for our tools, your email if you request Pro access or contact support, and a hashed (not raw) version of your IP address used to enforce free-tier usage limits.

How we use it

To generate the audio/text output you request, to enforce free-tier limits fairly, to send license keys and support replies, and to improve the service. We do not sell your data.

Third parties

We use Microsoft Edge's and Google's text-to-speech services to generate audio, Resend to send transactional email, and Adsterra to serve ads to free-tier users. Each has its own privacy practices.

Data retention

Uploaded audio for tool processing is not stored beyond the request. Usage records are pruned after roughly 2 months. License and account data is kept as long as needed to provide the service.

Your choices

Contact us (see Contact) to request deletion of your data.
