Tips for natural-sounding results

  • Punctuate normally — commas and periods create natural pauses without needing markup tags.
  • Use Markup mode's [pause:500ms] for a deliberate beat, or [slow]…[/slow] to emphasize a key phrase.
  • Markup mode also has expression presets — [excited], [cheerful], [calm], [serious], [sad] — that combine pace, pitch and volume to approximate a tone. They shift delivery noticeably but aren't true emotional acting.
  • For Urdu/Hindi scripts, write in the native script (Nastaliq/Devanagari) rather than transliterating into Latin letters — pronunciation is far more accurate.
  • Preview the voice on a short line before generating a long script, to confirm pacing and tone fit your content.

Frequently asked questions

Is the free tier enough for regular use?

The free tier includes a daily generation limit and monthly character quota — shown above the script box. It's enough for occasional use; frequent or long-form creators typically move to Pro.

What's the difference between Pro and Pro+?

Pro raises the TTS character quota substantially and removes daily generation caps. Pro+ adds higher TTS quota plus voice cloning and AI music generation (with monthly limits). See pricing for the exact numbers.

Can I use generated narration commercially?

Yes — narration you generate is yours to use in your own videos, courses, or projects, subject to the Terms of Service.

Do I need an account to try Voice Studio?

No. The free tier works immediately with no signup. Create an account only if you want Pro limits or Pro+ features like voice cloning.

How good is Urdu and Hindi compared to English?

Urdu and Hindi are first-class in the product, not buried add-ons. Always preview a line that includes names and numbers from your niche. See Urdu TTS and Hindi TTS.

Studio vs voice cloning — which should I use?

Use Studio for stock neural voices and fast multilingual narration. Use voice cloning when you need a specific reference voice identity (Pro+).

Can I redub an existing video instead of typing a script?

Yes — Video redub (Pro) transcribes, translates, and replaces the spoken track while keeping the picture.

What should I do after generating audio?

Common next steps: normalize loudness, trim, merge sections, or convert format for your editor.
