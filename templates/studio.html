Tips for natural-sounding results
  • Punctuate normally — commas and periods create natural pauses without needing markup tags.
  • Use Markup mode's [pause:500ms] for a deliberate beat, or [slow]…[/slow] to emphasize a key phrase.
  • For Urdu/Hindi scripts, write in the native script (Nastaliq/Devanagari) rather than transliterating into Latin letters — pronunciation is far more accurate.
  • Preview the voice on a short line before generating a long script, to confirm pacing and tone fit your content.
Frequently asked questions
Is the free tier enough for regular use?

The free tier includes a daily generation limit and monthly character quota — shown above the script box. It's enough for occasional use; frequent or long-form creators typically move to Pro.

What's the difference between Pro and Pro+?

Pro removes free-tier limits on text-to-speech. Pro+ additionally unlocks voice cloning and AI music generation. See pricing for details.

Can I use generated narration commercially?

Yes — narration you generate is yours to use in your own videos, courses, or projects.
