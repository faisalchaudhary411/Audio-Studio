Frequently asked questions
How does manual payment (EasyPaisa/JazzCash/bank) work?

Select your plan on the upgrade page, send the PKR amount shown to one of the listed accounts, then submit the form with your payment details. Access activates automatically for a grace period while we verify the payment, and becomes permanent once confirmed.

What happens if my manual payment isn't verified in time?

Temporary access granted while awaiting verification will stop working if we haven't confirmed the payment within the grace period. If that happens and you did pay, contact support with your reference ID — this is usually a delay on our end, not a rejection.

Can I switch between Pro and Pro+?

Yes — submit a new request for whichever plan you want on the upgrade page.

Is my license tied to one device?

Yes, a Pro/Pro+ license is tied to a single device at a time. If you get a new device or need to move your license, contact support.

Do you offer refunds?

Refund requests are handled case by case — contact us with your request ID and we'll take a look.

What's included in the free tier?

Text-to-speech with a daily generation limit and monthly character quota, plus access to all the free audio tools (transcribe, convert, merge, trim, denoise, voice effects, video-to-audio) with their own daily limits. No signup required.
